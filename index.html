About this VIT-AP time table planner

Winter Planner is a free time table planner for the VIT-AP FFCS winter semester 2026–27 registration. It keeps every theory and lab section of the 25 courses in the catalogue — slot code, room and instructor — so a clashing week can be spotted before the VTOP window opens.

Pick one theory slot and one lab slot per course and the planner blocks any section that overlaps another, then draws the week on VIT-AP's own Tue–Sat slot matrix. Everything stays in your browser: no account, no upload, nothing to sign up for.

Common questions

Is this the official VIT-AP time table?
No. Winter Planner is a student project, not affiliated with VIT. It copies the sections published in the official slot timetable and VTOP registration lists so you can plan beforehand — always confirm and register on VTOP itself.
Do I need to log in to use it?
No account, no login and no upload. The planner runs entirely in your browser, and your picks are stored in that browser's local storage, so nobody — including us — can see your timetable.
How do I pick a slot?
Every course takes one theory slot and one lab slot. Open a course card, tap the slot you want and the planner draws it on the weekly matrix; a second theory slot or a second lab slot replaces the first, and any overlap with another course is blocked.
Which VIT-AP semester does this cover?
The winter semester 2026–27 FFCS registration, with classes Tuesday to Saturday. Slots, rooms and instructors come from the official Winter 2026-27 slot timetable and the registration print-outs.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. The catalogue, the weekly matrix and the export buttons work from a 320 px screen upwards, in light or dark mode, and the page keeps working once it has loaded.
Can I export or share the timetable?
You can copy the plan as text, save it as an image or print it as a PDF from the timetable view. Sharing the plan is manual — nothing leaves your device automatically.
